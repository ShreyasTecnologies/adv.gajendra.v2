FUNCTIONALITY
- Responsive mobile navigation
- Phone, email and WhatsApp links
- Contact form validates required fields and prepares a WhatsApp message (user reviews and sends it)
- EMI calculator works in the browser
- Dismissible announcement banner
- Footer year updates automatically
- Vercel static-site configuration, robots.txt and sitemap.xml

IMPORTANT SETUP BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Replace YOUR-DOMAIN.example in sitemap.xml and robots.txt with the actual domain.
2. Verify the exact office map pin, business address, phone numbers, emails and opening hours.
3. Update the announcementMessage in script.js for festival greetings, office closures and filing reminders. This is manual, not an automatic festival calendar.
4. The contact form does not store submissions or send them to a server. It opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message to the supplied business number. For a database-backed form, connect a secure form service/backend.
5. Review the service descriptions, advocate designation, legal notices, privacy policy, terms and disclaimer with the responsible professional before launch.
6. Upload the full project folder to Vercel. Keep index.html, style.css, script.js, assets/, and all other pages at the project root.

No backend, admin dashboard, appointment booking, payment processing or automated filing is included. The website is a working static site with browser-side interactions and direct contact routes.
