FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers to common queries.

What should I bring for an income-tax return enquiry?

Depending on your circumstances, commonly relevant records may include PAN, Aadhaar details, Form 16, bank statements, details of other income and investment or deduction proofs. The exact list depends on your income sources and return type.

What information is useful for a GST enquiry?

Keep your business constitution and address details, PAN, turnover information, nature of business and any GST registration or notice details available. The precise requirements depend on the service being discussed.

Can I enquire about a tax notice?

Yes. Note the issuing authority, notice reference, date received and response deadline. Contact the office promptly and share the notice through an appropriate private channel.

Does the consultancy guarantee loan approval?

No. Loan approval, terms, interest rates and disbursement are decided by the lender after its own eligibility and risk assessment.
